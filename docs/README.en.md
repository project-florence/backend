<h1 align="center">Florence API</h1>

<p align="center">
  <strong>Florence's API Server</strong> — Market data collection, AI-driven analysis, and virtual portfolio management.
</p>

---

Florence's backend service gathers market data such as BIST stocks, currency rates, precious metals, macroeconomic indicators, and the IPO calendar; analyzes news; and powers AI-driven reports and strategy simulations for the web interface.

## Features

- **📈 Market Data** — BIST stocks, currencies, precious metals, macro indicators, and IPO calendar
- **📰 News & Sentiment Analysis** — Dedicated news search and news sentiment scoring
- **📄 Report Engine** — Fundamental analysis, news sentiment, and strategy evaluation reports
- **🎮 Simulation Engine** — Strategy stress-testing and advanced simulations
- **💰 Virtual Portfolios** — Commission accounting, XU100 benchmarking, import/export
- **🔐 Secure Authentication** — Argon2 password hashing and JWT-based sessions
- **🛠️ Admin Panel** — Separate management app for announcements and content
- **⏰ Scheduled Tasks** — Daily price refreshes and maintenance jobs

---
